## 2024-05-24 - Optimize Top-K extraction
**Learning:** Found instances where a full `sort_by` (O(N log N)) was used just to extract the top-K elements in `src/memory.rs` and `src/concourse/function/mod.rs`, which is unnecessary and performs poorly on larger datasets. Also learned that `select_nth_unstable_by` partitions the top-K items but leaves them in an unsorted order, so if the caller relies on the extracted elements being sorted, a subsequent `sort_unstable_by` is needed. This reduces the time complexity from O(N log N) to O(N + K log K).
**Action:** Replaced `sort_by` with partial sorting `select_nth_unstable_by` (O(N)), followed by a `.truncate(k)` and a `sort_unstable_by` to quickly extract and sort the top-K elements. It's crucial to ensure `k > 0` before calling `select_nth_unstable_by` to avoid panics.
