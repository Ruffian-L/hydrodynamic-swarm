## How to tell signal from overfitting the narrative

Deep dives (Jason + Claude) own the full log archaeology. Working heuristics for anyone tuning:

1. **Tag requested that was not in the prompt set** — strong candidate signal (self-extension of the control channel). Log verbatim.
2. **Tag only echoing the system card** — weak; may be compliance / narrative.
3. **Force telemetry moves when memory is on vs off** (δ, F_s, path, KL if available) — geometric signal.
4. **Force flat / zero but prose talks about memory, workspace, feelings** — narrative risk; do not merge on that alone.
5. **Same behavior after full context death + reload of store** — continuity signal (north star).
6. **Behavior only when asked “what do you feel / are you aware”** — contaminated; use ask-gate rules from TCT spec §5.

Do not merge hydro → niodoo-live → TCT until these distinctions are clearer in the logs. Research first. Willy-nilly merge is off.
